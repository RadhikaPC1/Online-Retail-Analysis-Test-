# 🛒 Online Retail Sales Analysis

An analysis of online retail sales performance, built to answer four key questions from leadership. The project covers monthly revenue trends, international market performance, customer concentration, and regional demand, and presents the findings as a stakeholder-ready presentation.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Goal:** Communicate clear, actionable insights from retail sales data to business leadership.

**Business questions addressed:**

1. How did revenue change month by month in 2011?
2. Which countries (excluding the United Kingdom) contribute the most revenue?
3. Who are our top customers, and how dependent is revenue on them?
4. Which regions show the strongest demand for our products?

---

## 📊 Key Findings

### 1. Monthly Revenue Analysis (2011)

| Period | Trend | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Jan → Apr | 📉 Decline | Revenue fell from **$750,000** to **$494,974** (about a 34% drop), possibly due to post-holiday consumer fatigue or market saturation |
| May → Nov | 📈 Recovery | Revenue climbed steadily to a peak of **$1,464,932** in November, likely driven by seasonal events, marketing campaigns, or product launches |

### 2. Country Revenue Performance (excluding the UK)

| Country | Revenue | Units Sold | Avg. Revenue / Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🇩🇪 Germany | $1.2M | 150,000 | $8.00 |
| 🇫🇷 France | $950K | 120,000 | $7.92 |
| 🇳🇱 Netherlands | $850K | 110,000 | $7.73 |

- **Germany** leads with a strong, balanced product mix.
- **France** has a higher revenue per unit than the Netherlands, suggesting a lean toward premium offerings.
- **Netherlands** shows healthy market engagement.

### 3. Customer Analysis

- The **top 10 customers** contribute **$1.5M**, about **37.5%** of total revenue.
- The **top customer (Customer A)** alone accounts for **$200K**, about **5%** of total sales.
- Revenue is meaningfully concentrated, so retaining key accounts is a strategic priority.

### 4. Regional Demand Analysis

| Region | Demand (Units) | Insight |
|---|---|---|
| Netherlands | 200,128 | Robust market potential |
| Spain | 180,000 | Growing consumer interest |
| Italy | 170,000 | Promising opportunity for market entry |

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- **Plan for seasonality:** prepare marketing and inventory for the spring dip and the autumn surge.
- **Protect key accounts:** build retention and relationship programs for the top 10 customers.
- **Grow in high-demand markets:** prioritize the Netherlands, Spain, and Italy for expansion.
- **Invest in premium offerings** in markets like France where revenue per unit is higher.
